Subject: [PATCH RFC 00/12] X1E80100: start the ADSP and CDSP from Linux at EL2

Linux can run at EL2 on X1E80100 laptops (slbounce), without the
Qualcomm hypervisor (QHEE/Gunyah). Besides KVM, this is what works on
X1E devices with more than 32 GB of RAM, where the EL1 setup under the
hypervisor has problems [TODO: link to the report].

The DSPs are the missing piece there. TZ PAS alone cannot start them:
QHEE sets up clocks, IOMMU apertures and a second (DTB) image around the
TZ calls. Today people work around this with qebspil, a UEFI driver that
starts the DSPs before Linux. This series lets qcom_q6v5_adsp start and
stop both DSPs itself, the way it already does on SDM845 and SC7280:
copy the images into the carveouts and release the core through the
QDSP6SS boot FSM. TZ is used once, to stop the ADSP image UEFI leaves
running.

What X1E80100 needs beyond the SC7280 path, all of which QHEE does
otherwise:

- a DTB image per DSP, and a descriptor for it at qdsp6ss+0x60 that a
  reset clears;
- the Q6 core PLL and core RCG, and the LPASS TCM branch clocks;
- stop sequences ending in the PDC sync reset and AOSS restart pulse of
  qcom_adsp_shutdown(), so patches 4-6 and 8-9 add X1E80100 to the PDC
  and AOSS reset drivers and describe the TCSR halt block;
- the CDSP runs in a Linux IOMMU domain, so the apertures QHEE grants it
  are mapped: the 103 of the ASUS build plus four read-only SPMI windows
  that newer QHEE builds add. Bisected on hardware, 19 are needed to boot,
  the rest (LLCC, BWMON, ...) once the NPU does any work, so all are kept.

The sequences were recovered from the QHEE binary (.367) and the
register names from the hardware region directory in the DSP firmware.
Where only the block is known, the offset is used as the name.

Patches 1-3 are prerequisites for EL2 that are not mine (1-2 from the
jglathe tree) and are only here so the series applies; they need their
own submission.

Questions:

1. The Q6 core PLL and RCG live inside QDSP6SS and lose their state on
   every DSP reset, and the CDSP's are only reachable after the boot FSM
   powers the core. Like the QDSP6SS CBCRs this driver already writes on
   SDM845, they are programmed from the remoteproc driver. Would a clock
   provider with a .prepare that reprograms the PLL be preferred?
2. The ADSP stop powers down the LPASS core and audio clock domains,
   including the audio PLLs that the LPASS macro drivers use. Should that
   part move to the LPASS clock/power drivers?
3. TCSR halt block compatible: "qcom,x1e80100-tcsr" is taken by the TCSR
   clock controller, so this adds "qcom,x1e80100-tcsr-halt".

Tested on an ASUS Vivobook S15 (S5507QA) with linux-msm/laptops-kernel
qcom-laptops: both DSPs start from cold boot, restart (CDSP with audio
loaded; ADSP restart with the audio stack loaded needs more work in the
audio drivers), battery and audio work, llama.cpp runs on the NPU.
Only one device; testing on others is very welcome.
